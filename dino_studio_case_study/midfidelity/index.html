Tyrannosaurus

Carnivorous Large theropod Late Cretaceous 12 m long

Description

One of the largest land carnivores of all time, Tyrannosaurus had a powerful bite and small, two-fingered forelimbs. Fossils show it hunted and scavenged across what is now North America.

When lived

Late Cretaceous, 68–66 million years ago

Excavation sites

14 recorded sites, mostly in the Hell Creek and Lance Formations, USA.

Taxonomy

Dinosauria Saurischia Theropoda Tyrannosauridae
