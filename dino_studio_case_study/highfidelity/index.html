Aardonyx

Description

Aardonyx is known from 2 immature individuals. Adults would have been much larger, probably over 10m. Aardonyx would have been largely bipedal but also capable of walking on all 4 legs — a transitional feature between basal sauropodomorphs and the larger sauropods that came later.

When lived

Early Jurassic, 199–189 million years ago
