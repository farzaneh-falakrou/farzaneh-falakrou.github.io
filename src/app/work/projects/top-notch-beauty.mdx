## Overview

The brief was deceptively simple: help people find beauty advice they can actually trust. Getting there required understanding why they didn't trust existing platforms — which meant 6 user interviews, a competitive teardown of Sephora and Intro, and two personas to keep the design honest throughout.

The work covered expert discovery and profile screens, a virtual try-on feature (camera or uploaded photo), an appointment booking flow, and personalised recommendation screens. Information architecture came from an open card sort with 5 participants, which kept the navigation from being shaped by assumptions.

Two rounds of usability testing — mid-fidelity with 6 participants, then high-fidelity with 5 unmoderated remote sessions — caught the gaps that looked fine in Figma but broke down with real users. Findings were prioritised using a rainbow spreadsheet and Nielsen's severity scale, so each iteration addressed the right problems in the right order.
