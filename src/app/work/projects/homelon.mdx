## Overview

First-time property buyers don't have a knowledge gap — they have a trust gap. They're drowning in listings, afraid of missing something, unsure which details actually matter. Homelon was designed to close that gap.

Starting from a competitive audit of Trulia, ImmoScout, and Zoopla, I mapped what the category got right and where it left buyers stranded. Five user stories drove the design: search, saving, comparison, property detail, and agent contact. The one that shaped the product most was Favourite & Compare — a side-by-side view across size, price, facilities, and energy rating, designed to replace the spreadsheet habit without making users feel like they needed one.

Visually, the design started with a mood board and settled on a watermelon-inspired palette — Coral Red, Sea Turtle Green, and Charcoal — that felt approachable without being frivolous. That palette became the foundation of a full style guide carried across mobile, tablet, and laptop.
