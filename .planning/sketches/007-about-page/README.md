# Sketch 007: Full About Page

## Design Question
The home page has an About teaser. This is the full page it links to. Three approaches, each with different voice and pacing.

## How to View
Open `.planning/sketches/007-about-page/index.html` in a browser.

## Variants

### A · Editorial Profile
Long-form magazine-style. Centered hero with italic display headline, portrait + caption row, narrative bio body with pull quotes and dropcap, structured timeline, dark "Currently" block, closing contact.

- **Reads like:** A profile in a design magazine
- **Pros:** Most personality, most voice, most distinctive
- **Cons:** Writing-heavy — every sentence has to earn its keep
- **Best when:** You're confident about the writing & want About to feel like an essay

### B · Structured Two-Column
Mirrors the case study template. Big italic hero. Portrait full-width. Then two-column body: bio with numbered sections on the left, sticky metadata sidebar on the right with Currently / Tools / Languages / Reading / etc.

- **Reads like:** A bio page on a senior designer's portfolio (Karri Saarinen style)
- **Pros:** Consistent with case study template structure. Easy to scan. Hiring managers love it.
- **Cons:** Less narrative than A; more "CV in design clothes"
- **Best when:** You want craft + clarity, not necessarily storytelling

### C · Conversational Journey
Letter-style — "Hi, I'm Farzaneh." Hero with greeting + portrait side-by-side, then chapters telling the architect→designer story with photos. Dark "Currently" block mid-page. Compressed timeline at the end.

- **Reads like:** A personal letter or substack post
- **Pros:** Warmest. Most narrative. Easiest to read for non-designers.
- **Cons:** Risks feeling self-indulgent if writing isn't tight; informal voice may not match hiring expectations
- **Best when:** You want About to feel like a conversation, not a CV

## What to Look For
- Voice match: which sounds like the same person who wrote the home page?
- Pace: how does each one feel to read top-to-bottom?
- Photo treatment: is the portrait inline (A), full-width (B), or paired with hero (C)?
- Maintainability: which one will you actually update over time?

## Content Slots Each Variant Includes
- Big italic display headline
- Portrait
- Bio narrative
- Career timeline
- "Currently" block (reading, listening, looking for)
- Tools / languages / awards
- Contact CTA
