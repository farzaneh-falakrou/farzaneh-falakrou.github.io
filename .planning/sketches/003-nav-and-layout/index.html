A note from the designer

On designing products
that feel right.

Most digital products feel assembled. Stitched together from templates and trend reports. My work is the opposite of that — slow, considered, often-times stubborn about details that look small but compound.

Five years across EdTech, PropTech, and consumer. What follows are four selected projects from the past three years.

Section 03 / About

I started as an architect — which is to say I still think in plans, sections, and elevations.

Designing software felt like a natural extension of designing buildings: both are about shaping how people move through a space, both reward thinking in systems, and both fail in the same way — when surface beauty hides a structure that doesn't hold.
