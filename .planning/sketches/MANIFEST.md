# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
UI/UX portfolio for Farzaneh Falakrou. Target audience: in-house product teams at tech companies. Goal: communicate "thinks deeply AND has strong taste" in the same breath. The site itself is the design argument.

## Reference Points
Established designer portfolios spanning four design lineages:
- **Design-engineer:** Rauno Freiberg (rauno.me), Brian Lovin (brianlovin.com)
- **Editorial:** Frank Chimero (frankchimero.com), Pentagram, NYT Magazine
- **Swiss/International Style:** Karri Saarinen (karrisaarinen.com), Müller-Brockmann, Aristide Benoist
- **Signature/Personality:** Tobias van Schneider (vanschneider.com), Jason Yuan (jasonyuan.design)

## Research Findings
- Quality over quantity — 2-3 deep case studies beat 10 shallow ones
- Process visibility matters — hiring managers want to see HOW you think
- 2025 trend: typography IS the design (bigger, bolder, more confident)
- Minimalism with impact — strip back, then make remaining elements dominate
- Personal touches stand out but must feel intentional, not decorative

## Locked Structural Decisions

- **Visual language:** Signature (sketch 002 · D) — cream + Instrument Serif + orange accent
- **Navigation:** Floating Dock (sketch 003 · B) — pill nav centered-bottom, hides on scroll-down
- **Selected work treatment:** Asymmetric Split (sketch 004 · D) — full-width zig-zag rows with preview, name, italic description, tag chips, and stat highlight
- **Case study template:** Editorial + Structured (sketch 005 · E) — editorial centered hero + cover, then a horizontal metadata strip (scrolls away), then 2-column body with sticky TOC on the left and a wider editorial article column on the right. No right sidebar. Section marks combine editorial label with structural number.
- **About page:** Conversational Journey (sketch 007 · C) — letter-style greeting hero with portrait, narrative chapters telling the architect→designer story with photos, dark "Currently" block mid-page, compressed timeline at the end.
