— About

I started as an architect —
which means I still
think in structure,
not just surface.

Hi, I'm Farzaneh, a UX/UI Product Designer with a background in architecture.

Architecture taught me to design for how people actually move through a space, and to lead teams doing it. When I moved into product design, the same instinct carried over: user-centred solutions, not decoration, interfaces that hold up under real use, not just in a mockup.

My interest in UX started during a Service Design course at Politecnico di Milano, exploring where urban design, technology, and citizen engagement meet. I followed that into a UX/UI Specialization at CareerFoundry, then designed digital products end to end across EdTech, FinTech, and consumer apps.

Beyond product design, I'm really into art: I love spending time in galleries and museums. Art not only fascinates me but also influences my creative approach. When not immersed in design, you might catch me nurturing a small jungle of plants, cultivating a peaceful and artful haven within my space.
