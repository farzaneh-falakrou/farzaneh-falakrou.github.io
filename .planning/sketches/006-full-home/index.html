— About

I started as an architect —
which is to say I still
think in plans, sections,
and elevations.

Designing software felt like a natural extension of designing buildings: both are about shaping how people move through a space, both reward thinking in systems, and both fail in the same way — when surface beauty hides a structure that doesn't hold.

Today I lead product design work for institutional clients in EdTech, PropTech, and consumer. I write occasionally about the parts of the work that don't fit in a case study.
